## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-03

### Added

- Pedagogical PyTorch categorical HMM sampling and inference.
- Exhaustive HMM and Influence Model oracles for tiny correctness proofs.
- Loop-based probability-space and stable log-space forward-backward algorithms.
- Literal expected-count Baum-Welch and a separate autograd HMM estimator.
- Structured Dong–Pentland Influence Model with exact product-state inference.
- Clearly labeled factorized marginal inference and network extraction.
- Independently reconstructed public MIT six-chain experiment parameters.
- Equation-led tutorial sequence and historical-fixture provenance boundary.
- Typed `latentlinks` package, tests, and `uv`-based packaging and release tooling.
